Managing a **$10,000/month Google Ad Grant** is an incredible growth lever for non-profits, charities, and 501(c)(3) organizations. However, Google enforces strict policy requirements that catch many non-profit directors off guard. A sudden drop in Click-Through Rate (CTR) below 5% or unapproved conversion goals can lead to automated account suspension.

In this comprehensive audit guide, we break down the exact checklist used by **AcquireLift** to audit non-profit Google Ads accounts, eliminate wasted ad credit, and maintain 100% policy compliance.

---

## 1. The 5% CTR Compliance Mandate

Google requires all Google Ad Grant accounts to maintain a minimum **5% account-level Click-Through Rate (CTR)** each calendar month. Failing this rule for two consecutive months results in account suspension.

### Key Audit Items:

- **Pause Low-CTR Keywords**: Filter your keywords by performance over the past 30-90 days. Any non-converting keyword with a CTR below 3% should be paused or replaced.
- **Eliminate Single-Word Keywords**: Google explicitly prohibits single-word keywords (e.g., "charity", "donate", "help"), except for approved brand names or emergency medical terms.
- **Remove Generic Keywords**: Broad, non-specific terms like "free books", "today news", or "job search" waste budget and trigger compliance flags.
- **Ensure Quality Scores > 2**: Any active keyword with a Quality Score of 1 or 2 must be removed immediately to protect account health.

---

## 2. Structural Account Requirements

Google Ad Grants cannot be structured like standard commercial Google Ads accounts. Google mandates specific structural parameters for every active campaign:

1. **At Least 2 Ad Groups per Campaign**: Every campaign must contain at least two active ad groups with closely themed keywords.
2. **At Least 2 Responsive Search Ads per Ad Group**: Each ad group must feature at least two distinct, active ads with high strength ratings.
3. **At Least 2 Sitelink Ad Extensions**: Sitelinks must direct users to distinct, relevant pages on your non-profit website.
4. **Specific Geo-Targeting**: Your ads must only target locations where your non-profit actively provides services or recruits supporters.

---

## 3. GA4 Conversion Tracking Setup

Google Ad Grants is not just about driving clicks — Google requires non-profits to generate meaningful user actions on their website.

### What Counts as a Valid Non-Profit Conversion?

- **Online Donations**: Completed donation thank-you page views or custom GA4 purchase events.
- **Volunteer Applications**: Form submissions for volunteer signup.
- **Newsletter Subscriptions**: Completed email opt-in confirmations.
- **Resource Downloads**: PDF downloads of annual reports, research guides, or educational materials.

> **Warning**: Pageviews with no engagement duration or generic page hits do _not_ qualify as valid conversion goals under Google Grant rules.

---

## 4. Smart Bidding & Budget Utilization

Many non-profits struggle to spend their full **$10,000/month** allowance ($329/day). Because Google caps manual CPC bids at $2.00 for Ad Grant accounts, manual bidding often limits reach.

### How to Unlock Maximum Spend:

- **Switch to Smart Bidding**: Utilize _Maximize Conversions_ or _Target CPA_ bidding. Smart Bidding allows Google to exceed the $2.00 CPC cap when an auction shows high conversion intent.
- **Expand Long-Tail Intent Keywords**: Target 3-word and 4-word intent-driven phrases (e.g., _"how to volunteer for youth mentorship in Chicago"_).
