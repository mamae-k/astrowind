Google Ad Grants now allows eligible nonprofits to run **Performance Max (PMax)** campaigns alongside traditional Search campaigns. This unlocks access to inventory across Google Search, Maps, YouTube, Gmail, and Display networks — all under your **$10,000/month free ad credit**.

---

## Key Benefits of PMax for Nonprofits

1. **Multi-Channel Visibility**: Reach supporters looking for volunteer opportunities or cause awareness across YouTube, Gmail, and Google Search.
2. **AI-Driven Creative Asset Grouping**: Combine headlines, images, and videos tailored to specific supporter personas.
3. **Automated Bidding Optimization**: Automatically allocates budget to the channels delivering the highest conversion rates.

---

## Best Practices for Ad Grant PMax Setup

- **Require Robust Conversion Data**: Do not launch PMax without active GA4 conversion tracking in place.
- **Provide High-Quality Visuals**: Use authentic photos of your team, volunteers, and mission projects instead of generic stock photos.
- **Set Up Audience Signals**: Feed GTM custom segment data (past website visitors, donor lists) into your PMax asset groups.
