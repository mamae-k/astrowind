Google requires all **Google Ad Grant** recipients to maintain an account-level **Click-Through Rate (CTR) of at least 5%** each calendar month. If an account falls below 5% for two consecutive months, Google automatically suspends ad serving.

While 5% sounds manageable, many nonprofit managers find their CTR hovering between 2% and 4% due to broad keyword selection and generic ad text.

At **AcquireLift**, the accounts we manage consistently achieve **8% to 15% CTR**. Here is our exact blueprint for keeping your Click-Through Rate well above Google's requirement.

---

## 1. Eliminate Generic & Single-Word Keywords

Single-word keywords (like *"charity"*, *"donate"*, or *"volunteer"*) trigger broad auctions where user intent is ambiguous. A user searching *"donate"* might be looking for plasma donation, clothing drop-offs, or blood drives.

### What to do instead:
- Target 3-word and 4-word **long-tail phrases** (e.g. *"where to donate winter coats in Seattle"*).
- Add strict **Negative Keyword lists** covering job searches, free downloads, and unrelated commercial terms.

---

## 2. Match RSA Headlines Directly to Search Queries

Responsive Search Ads (RSAs) give you up to 15 headlines and 4 descriptions. If your headlines don't dynamically mirror what the user typed into Google, your CTR will drop.

- **Headline 1:** Repeat the user's core search phrase (e.g., *"Volunteer Youth Mentor"*).
- **Headline 2:** Highlight your nonprofit's specific mission or impact.
- **Headline 3:** Direct call to action (e.g., *"Apply Online in 2 Minutes"*).

---

## 3. Leverage Dynamic Keyword Insertion (DKI)

Using Astro and Google Ads syntax, Dynamic Keyword Insertion allows your ad headline to automatically adjust to the exact search query entered by a user. This increases ad relevance and boosts Quality Score (which in turn lowers CPC costs and raises ad rank).
